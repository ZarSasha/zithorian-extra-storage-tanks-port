---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES ON SCALING OF RECIPE COST (last modified 2026-08-08)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recipe cost has been harmonized among several mods that I maintain, which are:

  Zithorian's Extra Storage Tanks For 2.0+ (v1.3.0+)
  Extra Storage Tanks: Tiny Storage tanks  (v1.6.0+)
  Extra Storage Tanks: Steel Storage tank  (v1.3.0+)

It has also been adapted to match the storage tanks in Krastorio 2, if that mod is installed.

By default, the ingredient cost will be roughly scaled according to this formular:

  All storage tanks: (Iron plate cost) = 4.6224 * (1k units of capacity) ^ (0.707),

where the last number is the inverse square root of 2, which means that the relative cost
gradually decreases with size. On the other hand, the proportion of steel required intentionally
increases, but not as accurately.

With Krastorio 2 installed, small and large storage tanks are scaled according to these formulas:

  Small storage tanks: (Iron plate cost) = 2.0544 * (1k units of capacity) ^ (0.707),
  Large storage tanks: (Iron plate cost) = 6.3    * (1k units of capacity) ^ (0.674),

where the parameters of the last formula is obtained through regression. The required proportion of
steel beams compared to steel plates tends to decrease, to fit the storage tanks of that mod.

So by default, the cost of each storage tank should roughly be:

  Vanilla:
    Storage tank       ( 25k):   45 iron plates (default)

  Zithorian:
    1x1 storage tank   (  3k):  ~10 iron plates
    2x2 storage tank   ( 12k):  ~27 iron plates
    3x4 storage tank   ( 72k):  ~95 iron plates
    5x5 storage tank   (150k): ~160 iron plates

  Extra Storage Tanks:
    Tiny storage tanks (2.7k):  ~10 iron plates
    Steel storage tank (125k): ~141 iron plates

With Krastorio 2 installed, the cost should instead be:

  Vanilla:
    Storage tank       ( 25k):   20 iron plates (small)

  Zithorian:
    1x1 storage tank   (  3k):   ~5 iron plates (small)
    2x2 storage tank   ( 12k):  ~12 iron plates (small)
    3x4 storage tank   ( 72k): ~109 iron plates (large)
    5x5 storage tank   (150k): ~183 iron plates (large)

  Extra Storage Tanks:
    Tiny storage tanks (2.7k): ~4.5 iron plates (small)
    Steel storage tank (125k): ~161 iron plates (large)

I end up using values which are the same, or are very close to, the above.
