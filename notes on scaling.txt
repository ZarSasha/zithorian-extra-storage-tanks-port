---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES ON SCALING OF RECIPE COST (last modified 2026-08-08)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recipe cost has been harmonized among several mods that I maintain, which are:

  Zithorian's Extra Storage Tanks For 2.0+ (v1.2.1+)
  Extra Storage Tanks: Tiny Storage tanks  (v1.6.0+)
  Extra Storage Tanks: Steel Storage tanks (v1.3.0+)

They are also adapted to match the steel storage tanks from Krastorio 2, if that mod is installed.

By default, the ingredient cost will be roughly scaled according to this formular:

  All storage tanks: (Iron plate cost) = 4.6224 * (1k units of capacity) ^ (0.707),

where the last number is the inverse square root of 2. The required proportion of steel also tend
to increase with storage capacity.

With Krastorio 2 installed, small and large storage tanks are scaled according to these formulas:

  Small storage tanks: (Iron plate cost) = 2.0544 * (1k units of capacity) ^ (0.707),
  Large storage tanks: (Iron plate cost) = 6.3    * (1k units of capacity) ^ (0.674),

where the parameters of the last formula is obtained through regression.

So by default, the cost of each storage tank should roughly be:

  Vanilla:
    Storage tank       ( 25k capacity):   45 iron plates (default)

  Zithorian:
    1x1 storage tank   (  3k capacity):  ~10 iron plates
    2x2 storage tank   ( 12k capacity):  ~27 iron plates
    3x4 storage tank   ( 72k capacity):  ~95 iron plates
    5x5 storage tank   (150k capacity): ~160 iron plates

  Extra Storage Tanks:
    Tiny storage tanks (2.7k capacity):  ~10 iron plates
    Steel storage tank (125k capacity): ~141 iron plates

With Krastorio 2 installed, they should instead be:

  Vanilla:
    Storage tank       ( 25k capacity):   20 iron plates (small)

  Zithorian:
    1x1 storage tank   (  3k capacity):   ~5 iron plates (small)
    2x2 storage tank   ( 12k capacity):  ~12 iron plates (small)
    3x4 storage tank   ( 72k capacity): ~109 iron plates (large)
    5x5 storage tank   (150k capacity): ~183 iron plates (large)

  Extra Storage Tanks:
    Tiny storage tanks (2.7k capacity):   ~5 iron plates (small)
    Steel storage tank (125k capacity): ~161 iron plates (large)

I generally end up with using values which are the same, or are very close to, the above.
